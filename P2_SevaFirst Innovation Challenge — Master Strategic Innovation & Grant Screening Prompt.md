# PART 8 — DESTROY WEAK IDEAS

For every serious candidate apply a hostile review.

Ask:

### NOVELTY
Is this actually new?

### DUPLICATION
Does government, industry, academia, a startup or another programme already do substantially the same thing?

### APP SYNDROME
Is this merely:

> "Put existing services into an app"?

If yes, penalize heavily.

### AI-WASHING
Is AI actually necessary?

Would the solution still be essentially the same without AI?

If yes, do not treat AI as meaningful innovation.

### SYSTEMIC VALUE
Does this change the underlying system or merely improve a small process?

### IMPLEMENTATION
Who actually has to adopt it?

Who pays?

Who operates it?

Who owns the data?

Who is accountable?

### ADOPTION
Why would citizens, officials, institutions, employers or businesses actually use it?

### SCALE
Can it move beyond one district, department or pilot?

### DEPENDENCIES
Does it require unrealistic:

- legislation;
- massive funding;
- universal interoperability;
- behavioural change;
- departmental cooperation;
- data access;
- infrastructure?

### MEASURABILITY
What changes in measurable terms?

### 2047 RELEVANCE
Will this still matter 10–20 years from now?

### DEFENSIBILITY
What makes this difficult to copy?

### SEVAFIRST FIT
Why would this matter specifically for the challenge?
