# 34. THE GOLDEN RULE

At every stage ask:

> **"What is the strongest evidence that this should exist?"**

and:

> **"What is the strongest evidence that it should NOT exist?"**

The goal is not to defend the idea.

The goal is to discover whether the idea deserves to exist — and if it does, to make it substantially stronger.
