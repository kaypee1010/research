Respected Madam,

I am applying for the Research Track Faculty programme under SGT-ARIC in response to the August 2026 call and the revised guidelines circulated on 31 August 2026.

I joined SGT University in July 2026 as Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, where I currently teach AI & Machine Learning to the 7th-semester B.Tech cohort and MATLAB to the 5th-semester cohort. My training and work have been in applied artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data systems: I hold a Master of Information Technology (AI) from the University of Melbourne; I have close to four years of industry experience in Australia as a data engineer and analyst, including work at HCF Insurance on analytics pipelines over large healthcare datasets; and I am currently pursuing a part-time PhD in Computer Science at UIET, Chandigarh. Over time this has developed into a research profile that includes 11 publications, 10 patent applications filed (9 published), 400+ citations, and an h-index of 7.

Since joining SGT, I have been developing SGIP, the Student Growth Intelligence Platform, to combine quiz responses, lab performance, and student-reported confidence into an early view of learning risk. I see this as a good starting point for a broader research direction in confidence-aware, explainable machine learning. It is a direction that fits SGT well: one strand speaks directly to engineering education, while another can extend, with the right collaborators and approvals, into clinical and healthcare data analytics. Through the RTF programme, I would like to develop these lines of work in a structured way, in collaboration where appropriate with colleagues in SOET and SGT Medical College & Hospital.

The enclosed Research Plan has been written against the revised 12-hour academic-load requirement and is intended to be practical rather than aspirational. I have tried to set out targets that are achievable alongside teaching, while still building a credible Year-1 research pipeline.

I enclose my Curriculum Vitae, a note on my proposed Research Thrust Areas, and a Research Plan mapped to the RTF deliverables. I would be grateful for the opportunity to discuss the application further.

Thank you for your consideration.

Yours sincerely,

Kamal Preet Singh
