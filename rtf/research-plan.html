Research Plan

Kamal Preet Singh · 12-Month RTF Deliverables Mapping (Year 1)

This plan maps my two research thrust areas onto the deliverables and points structure in the Revised RTF Guidelines (31 August 2026), which raise the academic-load requirement to 12 hours/week. I’ve built it around that load rather than around best-case assumptions. It leans on work I can start immediately — my existing publication pipeline, and the SGIP MVP once it’s sanctioned to run — and it deliberately doesn’t commit to a new patent filing or a third funding proposal this year, since either would add real workload on top of a full teaching commitment.

Risks & Dependencies

  • The SGIP pilot needs institutional budget sanction before it can run — mainly because the recommendation layer depends on LLM API calls, which cost money. Until that’s sorted, Thrust 1’s data-driven papers are on hold, which is exactly why the misinformation/NLP work matters as an immediate fallback.
  • The 12-hour teaching load is the other main constraint on this plan’s scope. It’s why I’m not committing to a new patent filing or a third funding proposal in Year 1 — both are realistic for Year 2, once the teaching schedule has settled in.
  • Thrust 2 depends on IEC/ethics approval and data-sharing access from SGT Medical College & Hospital — the item most likely to slip, which is why it’s scoped as a Year-1 proposal rather than a Year-1 pilot.
  • Publication counts also depend on journal review timelines, which I don’t control — the five-paper floor assumes normal delays; six to eight assumes a smoother cycle.
  • The V-Guard consultancy is a warm lead, not a cold approach — I’ve already delivered an invited lecture there — which is why I’ve treated it as close to secure for Year 1.

I’d welcome the Committee’s feedback on sequencing, and particularly on introductions within SGT Medical College that would help Thrust 2 move faster.
