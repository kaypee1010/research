Research Plan

Kamal Preet Singh · 12-Month RTF Deliverables Mapping (Year 1)

This plan maps my two research thrust areas to the deliverables and points structure in the Revised RTF Guidelines (31 August 2026). Targets are set against the revised academic load of 12 hours per week. Year-1 output will draw on the SGIP pilot, which has now been sanctioned, and on research work already in hand.

Risks & Dependencies

  • SGIP has been sanctioned, but the pilot must be set up and run with live classes before it produces research data. If it starts later than planned, the misinformation/NLP work keeps publication output on track.
  • Thrust 2 depends on IEC approval and data access from SGT Medical College & Hospital. This is the item most likely to take longer, so it is planned as a Year-1 funding proposal rather than a Year-1 pilot.
  • Publication counts depend on journal review times. The five-paper floor assumes normal delays; six to eight assumes a faster review cycle.
  • Research consultancy is not yet secured. It will be pursued through the R&D Cell in the first half of the year and is excluded from the floor.
  • With a 12-hour teaching load, Year 1 is limited to one patent filing and two funding proposals. A third funding proposal is planned for Year 2.

I would welcome the Committee’s guidance on sequencing, and on contacts within SGT Medical College who could help Thrust 2 move forward.
